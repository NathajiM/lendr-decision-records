# Decision Records

Eight records covering the decisions that most shaped Lendr.fi between 2023 and 2026.

## Format

Each record follows the same structure:

- **Status and date.** When the call was made and whether it held.
- **Context.** The situation and the binding constraint. A decision without a constraint is a preference.
- **Options.** What was genuinely considered, including options rejected early and why.
- **Decision.** The call.
- **Consequences.** What it bought and what it cost. The cost section is not optional.
- **What I would do differently.** Written with hindsight, marked as hindsight.

## Reading order

If you read three, read [0002](0002-whitelabel-platform-before-first-party-assets.md), [0003](0003-tiered-access-model.md), and [0006](0006-security-sequencing-under-a-fixed-audit-budget.md). Those cover a strategy reversal, a risk model for a product that touches securities law, and prioritization under a fixed budget.

[0001](0001-chainlink-ccip-over-layerzero-oft.md), [0004](0004-build-erc3643-subset-vs-vendor-t-rex.md), and [0005](0005-tenant-composition-clone-not-inherit.md) are the technical calls. [0007](0007-regulatory-strategy-as-a-product-surface.md) and [0008](0008-partnership-led-distribution.md) are the commercial ones.

## A note on honesty

Some of these decisions were driven by factors that are not purely technical: a partnership, a budget ceiling, a competitor's structural weakness. Where that is true the record says so plainly rather than reverse engineering a clean technical rationale. A decision record that only contains good reasons is a marketing document.
